About Juroom

Juroom forecasts the AAA national average price for regular gasoline. It is a GARCH–LSTM network that outputs a probability distribution for the next day. Juroom is “five” in Wolof, for Team 5.

Made by Team 5 of Applied Deep Learning (AAI 6640) at Northeastern University: Orville Rockwell Jr. (team lead), Yi-Ting Lin, Wenjun Chen and Yixin Tian.

Model and data. Juroom runs the midterm-project model (trained through Nov 3, 2026) and stays on it until the final version is added. The model itself is not retrained. Its data refreshes every morning from AAA, FRED and Kalshi, and each day’s forecast is frozen before the price is published.

It predicts one day at a time. For later days it carries the drift forward with a fade and widens the range at the rate measured on held-out days, so confidence drops the further out you ask.
